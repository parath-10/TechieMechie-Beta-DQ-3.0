One view of everything

All your channels in one clear picture

D2C brands run ads in one place, track sales in another, and check stock somewhere else. That's how money gets spent on products that are out of stock or quietly losing money.

Nexus brings ad spend, sales, inventory and product margins from Meta, Google, Amazon and TikTok into one clean view, so you see real profit before you spend the next rupee.
